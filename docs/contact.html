Frequently Asked Questions

"What vaccine records do I need to bring?"

We require proof of current Rabies and Bordetella vaccinations before your pet's grooming session.

"How long does a grooming appointment take?"

Standard baths and refreshes typically take 1.5 to 2 hours, while deluxe packages may take slightly longer.

"Can I stay with my dog during the bath?"

To keep pets calm and focused, we ask owners to drop off their dogs and pick them up once they are finished.
